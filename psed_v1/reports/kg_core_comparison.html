Material traversal — Al2O3

A · flat: 50 experiments deposit Al2O3, across 4 papers (10.1021_acs.jpcc.9b08176, 10.1039_d0cp03358h, 10.1063_1.5028178, 10.1116_1.4938104).

B · series: 50 experiments deposit Al2O3, across 4 papers (10.1021_acs.jpcc.9b08176, 10.1039_d0cp03358h, 10.1063_1.5028178, 10.1116_1.4938104).

Identical in both variants: Material is a shared node and ExperimentSeries sits above Experiment, so it does not change how a material reaches its experiments.

What ExperimentSeries changes

Variant B adds one node type between Paper and Experiment, built only from experimental_series_id — which the resolve layer mints solely for a discrete_experimental_sweep. It is not derived from figure number.

  • Grouping is partial by construction. 387 of 851 experiments belong to a sweep; the other 464 are not forced into singleton series and attach directly to their Paper.
  • Papers get far fewer direct children. A temperature sweep with 8 cases becomes one child of the Paper instead of eight, which is what makes a paper readable at a glance.
  • The sweep variable becomes explicit. Each series carries series_type, varied_quantity and member_count, so "this paper contains a deposition-temperature sweep" is a node property rather than something inferred by comparing sibling experiments.
  • Cost: +71 nodes and +142 edges over the flat model, and a second hop between Paper and Experiment.
