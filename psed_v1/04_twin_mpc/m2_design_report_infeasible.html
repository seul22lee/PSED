The range is measured by evaluating the twin at both ends of the effective-dose bracket, before any solving. A target outside it is reported as infeasible rather than iterated at. This verdict is for the resolved ratio only. Each operating family has its own bracket and its own achievable range, so section 3 may still find a feasible recipe at a different ratio — that is the point of generating candidates rather than inverting once. Here that happened: the target is outside the range at r = 1000 Pa/s, but the long_low_pressure family reaches it.
