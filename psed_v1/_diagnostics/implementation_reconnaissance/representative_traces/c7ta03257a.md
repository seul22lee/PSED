## The synthesis context is not lost

Material (`Pt`) and the ALD cycle count (`250`) both reach the entity and both persist into
`entities.json`, `results.json`, the canonical context pool and the full KG. What is absent
is any *object* representing the deposition to relate them to, because no entity in this
paper mints a case.

## Why `UnresolvedSourceEntity`

`entities.classify` returns `"unknown"`:
- Fig 7a: `unresolved_reason = "conflicting signals: ..."`
- Fig 8a: `unresolved_reason = "only one signal family (continuous_trace)"`

`ENTITY_CLASS["unknown"] = "UnresolvedSourceEntity"` (`entities.py:667`);
`CLASS_MODEL["unknown"]["is_experiment"] = False` (`entities.py:653-654`), so the gates at
`to_kb.py:1039` and `:1127` are not entered. The filter is by **class**, not by any
characterization-specific rule.

## Serialization capability today

- `performed_on` and `measures_case` are declared with the right endpoints and have no
  emitter.
- `build_kg.link(s, t, etype)` would emit either immediately — but it silently drops an edge
  whose endpoints are not both already in `nodes`, and no `Sample` or `Measurement` node is
  ever created.
- A resolved entity claiming `entity_class = "Sample"` would be typed `UnresolvedSourceEntity`
  by the `_ENTITY_NODE` fallback at `build_kg.py:304`, silently.

## `not_an_experiment` already preserves them as results

All four entities appear in `entities.json`, `results.json`, `canonical/curves.json` and the
full KG. They are excluded only from `experiments.json`, the core KG and the twin.
