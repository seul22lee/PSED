G. Corpus scope

ACTIVE8 = 182 cases across 8 papers. A development paper sitting in the same directory contributes 1 case and is excluded by roster rather than merged in — folding it into a headline number would make every count slightly about something the corpus excludes. Unseen papers are a separate evaluation domain and are never blended into these metrics.

A1. Species qualifier semantics

The ontology decides whether naming the chemical is part of identifying a quantity. Six quantities are qualified by: reactant — pulse_time, purge_time, partial_pressure, exposure, molecular_mass, precursor_molecular_diameter — and the role-prefixed composites the pipeline builds on them (precursor_pulse_time, coreactant_purge_time, carrier_gas_partial_pressure) inherit that qualifier through a suffix relation over ontology ids, so a new composite is covered the day it appears rather than when someone remembers to list it.

Previously every unattributed condition was reported unresolved, which made deposition_temperature = 200 °C incomparable to itself. Species absence is only missing information where the ontology asks for a species. Across active-8 shared conditions this removed 3378 false SPECIES_UNRESOLVED verdicts while retaining 1034 real ones. A stated species is still never discarded: an explicit reagent against a missing one stays unresolved on any quantity.

C1. Unit normalization and compatibility

Values are compared as physics rather than as printed text, through the existing dimension-aware unit model. 500 ms and 0.5 s are one value; 1 ms and 1 s are two, though both print as “1”; 200 °C and 473.15 K are one. Matching digits across dimensions are a coincidence, not an equality, so 1 s and 1 nm are NOT_COMPARABLE.

UNIT_CONVERTIBLE now requires units that actually convert: “short” s vs “long” ms is convertible- but-undecided, while “short” s vs “low” nm is not comparable at all — previously any two differing unit strings earned the convertible verdict. One helper serves comparison, sweep detection and the inventory, so the three cannot disagree about what “distinct” means.

A. Condition ontology semantics

The ontology already separates these; the axis layer had been collapsing them. pulse_time specialises time, carries SEC, and is qualified by reactant — a valve opens for one named chemical. exposure_time has no reactant qualifier and answers the broader question of how long a surface was exposed. An explicit axis rule was folding every “pulse length” into exposure_time, discarding exactly the reactant dimension species-aware comparison needs, and overriding records that had already said pulse_time. family still relates the two, so a comparison layer can generalise one to the other deliberately rather than by losing the distinction upstream.

C. The comparison model

The key is (quantity, species, process_step), compared value-wise through the existing unit layer. The rule for an unknown species is the identity rule this repository already runs on: MISSING is not SAME. A condition whose reagent was never attributed is not thereby the same control as one that was — it is reported SPECIES_UNRESOLVED rather than quietly matched or quietly dropped. This layer reads identity and never writes it; fingerprints and case IDs come from the semantic pipeline untouched.

F. Sweeps, found by identity rather than by label

G. Negative controls

The engine refuses false equivalence: pulse_time@SnI4 ≠ pulse_time@H2O even at identical value and unit; a named species is never matched to an unattributed one; bar and SiO2 appear nowhere as species (A2 hygiene holds); H2 flow ratio remains an unsupported quantity and so is never a comparable flow_rate condition (A0.1/A0.2 hold end to end).

H. Active-8 identity and structure

Unchanged: ResultSeries 231, points 4027, Measurements 213, SimulationRuns 34, DesignBranches 105, cases 25/66/2/11/44/7/7/20 = 182, DISTINGUISHED 130, INDISTINGUISHABLE 52. The pulse/exposure correction touched no active-8 axis, and the comparability layer writes nothing.

K. Deferred

Not attempted here, and each needs its own evidence: a natural-language query layer over these primitives; species-aware comparability of results rather than conditions; broader unit transformation; the flow_ratio ontology gap; electrode_potential, vapor_pressure, critical_angle.

Still open and material: in one paper the source states precursor purge durations that upstream extraction typed as pulse_time. The persisted evidence span for those conditions is truncated to “pulse and 10 s” — short of the word that would settle it — so the correction cannot be made from the artifacts and needs either re-extraction or a methods-recipe parser reading the source prose. That is new extraction infrastructure rather than a same-layer fix, and it would move 44 cases' fingerprints, so it is left visible rather than guessed.
