1 · What was repaired, and what was not

Repaired. represents_same_measurement_as now produces a real MeasurementAct grouping by transitive closure, so the model's own knowledge that three panels are one observing act is expressed in the structure rather than kept in a sideband. Case→Run links are scoped to the sample that carries them, so a case no longer implies that every realization was grown in one run. ResultSeries expose their complete case membership through cases_for_result_series, and a caller that wants one case must use single_case_for_series, which returns MULTI_CASE rather than an arbitrary member.

Not repaired, deliberately. No Sample was merged. The records carry no traceable specimen identifier, and the source's only same-sample statements concern a different figure, so merging rows 4/5/6 would mean inferring physical identity from equal conditions — the one inference the identity rules exist to forbid. Classified PHYSICAL_IDENTITY_NOT_EXTRACTED: an extraction gap, not an architecture defect.

Nothing scientific moved. Measurement ids, ResultSeries ids, points, canonical curves, case ids and fingerprints are untouched; an act is a grouping over existing ids, not a replacement for them.

2 · Condition Case is not an Experiment

An ExperimentalCase is a condition-defined case. Its conditions are nominal: they describe the design point, not something measured on any particular chip. Case conditions are therefore offered to a realization as CASE_CONTEXT, never rewritten as sample evidence.
