II A ('1711 unique thermal ALD processes for 539 thin film materials') and Table I columns Material, Reactant A, Reactant B; Cremers 2019 Sec.     Note: schema-miner splits this across ALDSystem (ALDMethod, MaterialDeposited) and ReactantSelection (Precursor, CoReactant). carrier_gas, which schema-miner lists under ReactantSelection, sits under deposition_run: Cremers Sec.     entries: process_mode, material, reactant_A, reactant_B
  • process_parameters: Settings chosen by the experimenter for a deposition, Cremers' own term: the settings of each step of the ALD cycle and the settings of the whole run. [Cremers 2019 Sec. III B ('The process parameters were identical for both substrates: T = 100 C, P_TMA = 0.28 Pa, and P_H2O = 0.25 Pa.     • cycle_step: Settings of one step of the ALD cycle: pulse and purge times, the reactant partial pressure, their product the exposure, the plasma power of a plasma step, and the time within a reactant pulse along which a trace is recorded. [Cremers 2019 Sec. I (the four steps of the ALD cycle), Sec. II D ('the product of the reactant partial pressure and the pulse time'), Sec. II E Eq. 9 (d theta / dt within a pulse), Sec. IV C ('the gas pressure, the RF power, the plasma exposure time')]
      entries: pulse_time, purge_time, partial_pressure, exposure, plasma_power, time, plasma_gas_ratio, cycle_time
    • deposition_run: Settings that hold for the whole deposition run: deposition temperature, reactor pressure, number of cycles, carrier gas and its flow, and precursor source temperature. [Cremers 2019 Sec. III B ('T = 100 C', '1000 ALD cycles were applied', 'in the same ALD run'), Tables II-IV column 'T (C)', Sec. II C (reactants entrained in an inert carrier gas); Popov 2025 Sec. IV ('source temperature')]
      entries: carrier_gas, deposition_temperature, process_pressure, source_temperature, cycle_number, gas_flow_rate, precursor_purity, annealing_temperature
