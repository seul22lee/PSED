2b. Hierarchy

  • ald_process: What is deposited and from what: the film material, its reactants A and B, and the process type (thermal, ozone-based, plasma-enhanced). Popov counts a process as a material plus its reactant combination; Cremers tabulates an experiment under 'Film (process)', e.g. Al2O3 (TMA/H2O). [Popov 2025 Sec. II A ('1711 unique thermal ALD processes for 539 thin film materials') and Table I columns Material, Reactant A, Reactant B; Cremers 2019 Sec. IV ('We distinguish three types of ALD processes: thermal, ozone-based, and plasma-enhanced') and Tables II-IV column 'Film (process)']
    Note: schema-miner splits this across ALDSystem (ALDMethod, MaterialDeposited) and ReactantSelection (Precursor, CoReactant). carrier_gas, which schema-miner lists under ReactantSelection, sits under deposition_run: Cremers Sec. II C treats the carrier gas as a choice of reactor operation, not of chemistry.
    entries: process_mode, material, reactant_A, reactant_B
  • process_parameters: Settings chosen by the experimenter for a deposition, Cremers' own term: the settings of each step of the ALD cycle and the settings of the whole run. [Cremers 2019 Sec. III B ('The process parameters were identical for both substrates: T = 100 C, P_TMA = 0.28 Pa, and P_H2O = 0.25 Pa. During the process, 1000 ALD cycles were applied with the following pulse/pump times'); Sec. IV ('the process parameters were not always reported')]
    • cycle_step: Settings of one step of the ALD cycle: pulse and purge times, the reactant partial pressure, their product the exposure, the plasma power of a plasma step, and the time within a reactant pulse along which a trace is recorded. [Cremers 2019 Sec. I (the four steps of the ALD cycle), Sec. II D ('the product of the reactant partial pressure and the pulse time'), Sec. II E Eq. 9 (d theta / dt within a pulse), Sec. IV C ('the gas pressure, the RF power, the plasma exposure time')]
      entries: pulse_time, purge_time, partial_pressure, exposure, plasma_power, time
    • deposition_run: Settings that hold for the whole deposition run: deposition temperature, reactor pressure, number of cycles, carrier gas and its flow, and precursor source temperature. [Cremers 2019 Sec. III B ('T = 100 C', '1000 ALD cycles were applied', 'in the same ALD run'), Tables II-IV column 'T (C)', Sec. II C (reactants entrained in an inert carrier gas); Popov 2025 Sec. IV ('source temperature')]
      entries: carrier_gas, deposition_temperature, process_pressure, source_temperature, cycle_number, gas_flow_rate
  • ald_reactor: Properties of the equipment: the reactor and its manufacturer, its class by working pressure, how reactants are delivered, and the configuration of the plasma source. [Cremers 2019 Sec. II C 'ALD reactor types' (pump-type, flow-type, AP-type; 'can easily be adapted to accommodate plasma sources'), Table IV ('plasma configuration'); schema-miner ProcessParameters/Reactor and DeliveryMethod]
    Note: Cremers Sec. IV C counts plasma configuration among 'the process parameters, such as deposition temperature, gas pressure, and plasma configuration'; it is placed here because it is a property of the plasma source, not a setting of a run. schema-miner lists Reactor under ProcessParameters; same reason.
    entries: reactor, delivery_method, reactor_type, plasma_configuration
  • test_structure: The substrate or test structure on which conformality is quantified, with its geometry (width w, depth L, extent z, aspect ratio, equivalent aspect ratio) and the position inside it along which a profile is recorded (distance, distance over width). [Cremers 2019 Sec. III 'Structures to quantify the conformality of ALD' and Table I; Sec. II G (AR = L/w, Eq. 11; EAR); Tables II-IV columns 'Substrate' and 'EAR'; Sec. IV ('film thickness as a function of the depth of the structure')]
    entries: structure, aspect_ratio, equivalent_aspect_ratio, feature_width, feature_depth, feature_extent, distance, dimensionless_distance
  • deposited_film: What is observed on the sample after deposition: the film growth, its conformality inside 3D features, and the properties of the film material. [Cremers 2019 Sec. II A (uniform films along a planar substrate, conformal films inside 3D features); schema-miner MaterialProperties ('Assessment of the properties of the deposited film'); Popov 2025 Sec. I ('properties of the deposited films, such as film thickness, morphology, conformality, and composition')]
    Note: schema-miner keeps GrowthPerCycle and nucleationPeriod under ProcessParameters/ThicknessControl; they are outcomes (deviation role_changed on the entries) and sit under growth.
    • growth: How much film is deposited and how it builds up: thickness, growth per cycle, nucleation period, and thickness variation over a planar substrate. [Cremers 2019 Sec. I ('After each ALD cycle, a certain amount of material (coating) is deposited on the surface, which is called the growth per cycle'), Sec. II A (uniformity); schema-miner ThicknessControl and Uniformity]
      entries: growth_per_cycle, nucleation_period, thickness_nonuniformity, film_thickness
    • conformality: Measures of how far and how evenly the film reaches into a 3D feature: normalized thickness profile, penetration depth, coated aspect ratio, step coverage. [Cremers 2019 Sec. II H 'Ways to express the level of conformality' (step coverage, PD50%, PD80%, coated (E)AR); Tables II-IV column 'Coated EAR']
      entries: coated_aspect_ratio, penetration_depth, step_coverage, normalized_thickness
    • film_properties: Optical, electrical, compositional and structural properties of the deposited film. [schema-miner MaterialProperties (OpticalProperties, ElectricalProperties, ChemicalComposition, Crystallinity, FilmDensity); Popov 2025 Sec. I ('properties of the deposited films')]
      entries: crystallinity, refractive_index, absorption_coefficient, resistivity, carrier_density, mobility, composition, film_density
  • modelling: The description of reactant transport and surface reaction used to simulate thickness profiles: the surface-reaction parameters and the models that use them. [Cremers 2019 Sec. V 'Simulation models on conformality of ALD', Sec. V C 4 'Reaction mechanism', Sec. V D 2 'Thickness profile and sticking coefficient']
    • surface_kinetics: Parameters of the lumped Langmuir description of the surface reaction: sticking and recombination probabilities, adsorption equilibrium constant, saturation growth per cycle, surface coverage. [Cremers 2019 Sec. II E 'Reaction mechanisms and sticking probability' (Eqs. 5-9: s0, theta, k_ads and k_des; recombination probability r); Sec. V C 4]
      entries: initial_sticking_coefficient, recombination_probability, surface_coverage, adsorption_equilibrium_constant, saturation_growth_per_cycle
    • models: Named transport-reaction models, analytical or computational, whose parameters are the surface_kinetics entries. [Cremers 2019 Sec. V A 'Overview' (1. Analytical models, 2. Computational models), Table VIII]
      entries: ylilammi_diffusion_reaction, gordon_plug_flow, yanguas_gil_markov_chain, arts_slope
