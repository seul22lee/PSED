5a. Form hashes. Each kb/records/<pid>.jsonl line carries ontology_hash (export.ontology_hash() at call time: ontology.yaml + units.yaml, the
source of form.json) and prompt_hash (common.request_hash: prompt + response schema + thinking). compile.py Run.read() takes the latest pass-1
line per section and the pass-0 line before them, whatever their ontology_hash; no reader in kb/extract, kb/pilot or ontology compares a
record's hash (grep: ontology_hash is written by common.run and compile.main's summary.csv, read nowhere). The only hash check is the
cache: common.plan / common.run look up cache/<pid>/<pass>_<section>_<request hash>_<model>.json, so a new form (a new schema) is a cache
miss and a new call. The three papers' records in use carry b8048d18… (2026-10-20); the current ontology hash is e98f546e…
(form_hashes). After P158 freezes a new form, compile.py will keep reading these lines and validate them against the new form.json
(step a): a record valid under the old form and not under the new one is dropped there, with the validator as its reason.
