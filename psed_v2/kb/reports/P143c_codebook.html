## a. Tree model (sources: kb/pilot/lists.py lines 122-125, NODE_TYPES / ACTIVITY_KINDS / COMPARISON_KINDS / IDENTITY_KINDS; TERMS; kb/pilot/facts.py retree() docstring)

lists.py:

```
tree rows (stage 3): the paper, activities (something done, with settings: kind from ACTIVITY_KINDS, order when several apply to one
entity, how stated or inferred) and entities (a physical thing or its state: a specimen, a piece cut from it, a position, a model
run; how stated or inferred). The tree is physical provenance only: paper -> recipe -> deposition -> entity -> later activities ->
facts. The authors' comparison intent is a fact of type comparison (kind, the varied quantity, links = purpose + members), not a parent.
```

- NODE_TYPES: paper, activity, entity
- ACTIVITY_KINDS: recipe, deposition, anneal, plasma_treatment, measurement, simulation
- COMPARISON_KINDS: series, grid, replicate
- IDENTITY_KINDS: different, not_stated, same

facts.py retree() (the placement rule):

```
Rebuild a paper's stage-3 tree under the activity/entity model from the old placement (process, runs, groups):
paper -> recipe activity (the old process) -> deposition activities -> entities -> measurement activities -> facts.
  deps:  {dep_id: dict(statement, label, how, evidence[, parent, kind])}: the deposition (or other) activities to create; parent
         defaults to the recipe.
  ents:  {old_run_id: dict(dep, ent, parent, statement, label, how, evidence[, via])}: each old run maps to an activity `dep`
         (its settings go there) and, unless ent is None (the run was itself a deposition), to an entity `ent` under `parent`
         (a deposition or another entity: a piece); `via` = (activity id, kind) puts the run's measurement activities under a
         later treatment activity of the entity (an anneal).
  sims:  {old_run_id: dict(act, ent, parent, statement, label, evidence)}: a simulation activity with its model-run entity.
  group_facts_to: {group_id: node_id}: facts under that group move to the node (a group's shared inputs to a simulation activity,
         a cell group's ratio setting to its deposition); drop_groups: group rows deleted (their members merged into one entity).
  extra: rows to add as (id, type, statement, parent, kw).
A setting with a measurement method, or any observation, derived or model fact under a run, goes under the entity's
measurement activity of that method (<key>:meas:<entity>:<method>; "measurement" when the fact names none); a setting without
one goes to the run's activity, or to the entity itself when several entities share that activity (the per-sample settings of
one run that coated three samples: an entity's own settings are its properties and count in its condition vector). Shared settings and observations under the old process with a measurement method go to
<key>:method:<method> (a measurement activity under the paper); the rest stay in the recipe. Group rows survive with
parent = paper and links = purpose + members (old member runs mapped to their entities or activities); the old process and run
rows are deleted.
```

Terms:

- **run**: one deposition the paper performs; specimens coated together share one run; separate depositions are separate runs even with the same chemistry, temperature and structure. (lists.py TERMS)
- **specimen**: one physical sample that is measured (a coated structure, a film, a substrate piece); it comes from one run. (lists.py TERMS)
- **input entity**: a thing that exists before any deposition (a substrate, a porous body), placed under the recipe and named in a deposition activity's inputs. (lists.py TERMS)
- **model run**: one set of inputs of a simulation whose result the paper reports (a curve, a table row). (lists.py TERMS)
- **series / replicate / grid (comparisons)**: never a parent and never asked of the extractor; the compiler derives them from the condition vectors of the experiment units — series: members differ in exactly one quantity; replicate: in none; grid: in two. A name the authors give such a set ("Series A") is kept as its label. (lists.py TERMS)
- **piece**: "a piece cut from it" (lists.py); "a deposition or another entity: a piece" (facts.py retree())
- **measurement activity**: "goes under the entity's measurement activity of that method (<key>:meas:<entity>:<method>; "measurement" when the fact names none)" (facts.py retree())
- **simulation**: "a simulation activity with its model-run entity" (facts.py retree())

## b. Layouts (source: ontology/export.py P143_TABLE_LAYOUTS, P143_PANEL_LAYOUTS: P143 definitions)

Table layouts:

- **row_per_unit**: Each row is one experiment unit (a run, a specimen or a model run); the columns are its conditions and results.
- **column_per_unit**: Each column is one experiment unit; the rows are its conditions and results.
- **cell_per_unit**: Each cell is one experiment unit: its row and column headers are its conditions, the cell holds its result.
- **not_units**: The rows are not experiment units: a symbol table (symbol, meaning, value) or a comparison with the literature (cited values).

Panel layouts:

- **curve_per_unit**: Each curve is one experiment unit; the panel compares units that differ in the conditions the legend names. Example: one curve per model run (Ylilammi Fig. 4).
- **panel_one_unit**: The whole panel is one experiment unit; its curves are several results of one measurement or one model run. Example: an EDS panel = one specimen, its curves one measurement's results per element.
- **point_per_unit**: Each point or grid cell is one experiment unit; the axes are its conditions, the value or colour its result. Example: a heat map whose grid points are runs (axes = conditions, colour = result).

## c. Record types, stances, NK reasons, the equation rule (sources: kb/extract/pass1_elements.py RULES; kb/pilot/lists.py STANCES and its comment; P143 equation rule in ontology/export.py)

Record types:

- setting: a condition the authors chose, including the measurement setup (objective, resolution, scan length, precision).
- observation: a measured result on a specimen.
- derived: a number the authors computed from their data (fit parameters, values derived in a table).
- model: an equation or a model statement; quantity = what the equation gives, variables = its symbols. An equation is a model, not
  a definition.
- definition: a symbol or term the paper defines; term = the symbol as printed, quantity = the entry it stands for when one exists.
- claim: a causal statement or an interpretation of results, with cause and effect (ids of the cause/effect enum), relation and
  stance; see the claim example below. An abstract, introduction or conclusion paragraph that states a result or an interpretation
  is a claim, never NK.
- caveat: a limitation or assumption the authors state about their own method or data (not a claim).
- cited: a value or statement the paper attributes to another work (not a setting).

Stances (asserted, hedged, open, limitation): "likely", "may" -> hedged; "cannot be excluded", "further work" -> open; a stated shortcoming of the study or method -> limitation; asserted: stated as a finding, without hedging words (P143b)


NK reasons:

- Answer every element id below, once: nk = none with its records, or, when the element holds no knowledge, nk = citation_line,
  boilerplate, duplicate (duplicate_of = the element id it repeats) or no_knowledge, with records empty:
  boilerplate: headings, author lines, acknowledgements; citation_line: a reference-list entry; duplicate: a statement already
  recorded from another element; no_knowledge: the element states nothing the form can hold.

Equation rule:

- model: an equation or a model statement; quantity = what the equation gives, variables = its symbols. An equation is a model, not
  a definition.
- variables (models and definitions): each symbol -> its quantity id, or null when no id fits.
- Symbol meanings come from the paper's symbol table and the paragraphs around the equation. (P143)
