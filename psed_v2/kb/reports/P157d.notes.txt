Choices made in this unit, each one reported, none made to pass the acceptance (the acceptance ran after them):
1. FIX 1 scope: quantity and categorical entries only. The symbol-like rule also selects 42 strings of species, structures and a mechanism
   (H2O, TiCl4, Al(CH3)3, PillarHall-4, LHAR4, ...; symbols_not_moved). A formula or a product name is a name in every paper; moved to a
   paper-local symbol with no recorded paper it would stop resolving species in compile step f and in a0. They stay aliases.
2. Symbol matching keeps case (export.symbol_key), as P156's norm_symbol (R is not r). Case-folded, feature_depth's L (paper yim20, from its
   evidence) would promote yim20's l (distance between rows of pillars) again (symbol_case).

   paper '' and promotes nowhere (49 of the 98: 48 aliases and step_coverage's SC, whose entry has no evidence; symbols_move, paper_from).
4. never.csv labels are read in their own format, 'label [unit]' (the text before a trailing ' [unit]', export._never_label, as check 12
   reads them). Without it, 5 rows matched by pilot labels ('rms thickness error [nm]', 'Viscosity of gas [µPa s]', 'Accelerating voltage
   [keV]') stopped being never. Lookup keys of ids, names and aliases are whole strings (no unit group stripped).

   group, the singular or plural of its last word). No plural aliases were added (an ontology decision); no row of the three papers changed by it.

   symbol), category 'Model internals not in Sec. 8', not through their round-1 symbol rows. Option: give the pilot's symbol-like never rows a
   paper (symbol yes).
