Claims and caveats

claim asserted: self_limiting_growth causes step_coverage gor03:abs.g1#1
complementary self-limiting surface reactions of two vapors let ALD coat the inside of narrow features such as holes with high conformality
→ holes, 30 × 10^3 Langmuir, 250 cycles
claim asserted: exposure requires step_coverage gor03:abs.g2#1
two conditions are necessary for a uniform coating in a hole: a minimum amount of each vapor (stoichiometry) and a minimum product of vapor pressure and exposure time at the hole entrance (kinetics)
(no card reached)
claim asserted: exposure requires step_coverage gor03:abs.g2#2
the theory agrees with the HfO2 data: holes of the final aspect ratio are coated completely once the exposure exceeds the Eq. (15) minimum
→ holes, 30 × 10^3 Langmuir, 250 cycles → holes, 4 × 10^3 Langmuir, 250 cycles → holes, 2 × 10^3 Langmuir, 350 cycles → Fig. 3 prediction
claim asserted: aspect_ratio increases exposure gor03:abs.g3#1
the theory gives scaling laws for other aspect ratios: the required amount of precursor grows linearly with the aspect ratio, the required exposure as its square
(no card reached)
claim asserted: self_limiting_growth causes step_coverage gor03:s3.p2#1
conformal CVD needs its rate-limiting step on the surface; ALD forces the two reactions onto the surface by supplying the reactants in alternating pulses, so it can coat particularly high aspect ratios
(no card reached)
claim asserted: surface_chemistry requires step_coverage gor03:s3.p5#1
the chemistry condition: each reactant must react self-limitingly, fast, completely and irreversibly on the surface left by the other, with no physisorption, no etching and no reaction with its own products; hafnium dialkylamides with water meet it
(no card reached)
claim asserted: exposure requires step_coverage gor03:s3.p5#2
the stoichiometry condition: the dose of each reactant must cover the whole exposed area, flat surface, hole interiors and reactor walls; bulky ligands keep the saturation dose below the number of reactive sites
(no card reached)
claim asserted: gas_diffusion limits step_coverage gor03:s3.p9#1
the kinetics condition: each dose must stay at the hole entrances long enough to react with the whole interior, the rate being set by diffusive molecular flow down the hole, not by the fast surface reactions
(no card reached)
claim asserted: aspect_ratio increases exposure gor03:s4.p9#1
the stoichiometric amount of reactant for conformal coating inside holes rises linearly with their aspect ratio
(no card reached)
claim asserted: gas_diffusion requires exposure gor03:s6.p1#1
even a stoichiometrically sufficient amount of precursor needs a minimum time to be deposited over a patterned substrate; that time is estimated from first principles
(no card reached)
claim asserted: initial_sticking_coefficient causes surface_coverage gor03:s6.p2#1
an ideal ALD reactant has a sticking coefficient of 1 on a surface saturated with the other reactant until it reaches its own saturation coverage, then zero
(no card reached)
claim asserted: initial_sticking_coefficient decreases exposure gor03:s6.p8#1
the calculated exposures assume a reaction probability of 1; a real reaction needs more, so they are lower limits
(no card reached)
claim asserted: initial_sticking_coefficient decreases exposure gor03:s6.p10#1
wherever the saturation exposure lies in that range, the Hf(NMe2)4 reaction is extremely fast
(no card reached)
claim hedged: gas_diffusion increases exposure gor03:s6.p11#1
diffusion and convection within the reactor volume lengthen the exposure time needed on a flat surface; reactor-scale modelling is needed for those delays
(no card reached)
claim asserted: film_thickness increases aspect_ratio gor03:s7.p14#1
the aspect ratio grows as the film narrows the hole, so the exposure criterion has to hold for the coated hole's final aspect ratio

claim asserted: aspect_ratio increases exposure gor03:s8.p6#1
for large aspect ratios the required exposure grows with the square of the aspect ratio while the required amount of precursor grows linearly
(no card reached)
claim asserted: exposure increases step_coverage gor03:s8.p2#1
HfO2 coatings made with exposures above the Eq. (15) minimum for the final aspect ratio had 100 % step coverage, in agreement with the calculation

claim asserted: exposure increases step_coverage gor03:s8.p4#1
exposures below the minimum gave incomplete step coverage
→ Fig. 3 prediction
caveat gor03:s7.p1#3
The model neglects precursor depletion outside the hole; the samples sat near the vapor entrance, and where depletion matters the post-reaction concentration is the conservative estimate.
→ Fig. 3 prediction
caveat gor03:s6.p8#2
The calculated saturation exposures assume a reaction probability of 1 and are lower limits; the real exposure is higher by the inverse of the reaction probability.
(no card reached)
caveat gor03:s6.p9#3
The 7 % reaction probability is a lower bound set by the size of the microbalance sensor; the true saturation exposure lies anywhere between 3 and 43 Langmuir.
(no card reached)
caveat gor03:s7.p14#2
The minimum exposure is calculated for the final aspect ratio of the narrowed, coated hole, not the as-etched one.
→ Fig. 3 prediction → etched holes in Si
caveat gor03:s8.p5#7
The thin light line along the edges of the uncoated holes is an imaging artefact of the electron microscope, not a film.
→ etched holes in Si
caveat gor03:s6.p7#8
The molecular mass of Hf(NMe2)4 assumes a monomeric vapor.
(no card reached)
caveat gor03:s6.p7#9
The flat-surface exposure is evaluated at 200 °C while the depositions ran at 150 °C.
(no card reached)
