 # Codebook
 
+Kinds (source: ontology/ontology.yaml kinds:, P155):
+
+```
+quantity:  something with a number: measured, set, fitted or computed; carries a unit (or "1" for dimensionless). Example: film_thickness, deposition_temperature.
+unit:      how a quantity's number is scaled; symbol, variants and conversion factor live in units.yaml. Example: nm, Torr.
+species:   a chemical thing: element, molecule, ion or radical, named as the paper names it. Example: TMA, H2O, O radical.
+structure: a physical object or shape the deposition acts on or produces: substrate, trench, pillar array, film, porous body. Example: lateral_high_aspect_ratio_channel.
+mechanism: a physical or chemical process the paper invokes to explain a result, not a number. Example: Knudsen diffusion, recombination.
+method:    how something was deposited, measured or computed; a measurement method is what makes a measurement activity. Example: spectroscopic_ellipsometry, xps.
+qualifier: a label that narrows a fact without being a quantity: which step, which stage, which statistic. Example: step=reactant_A, stat=mean.
+model:     a named mathematical or simulation model the paper fits or runs; its parameters are quantities with role model_parameter. Example: ylilammi_diffusion_model, elam_monte_carlo.
+```
