run: one deposition the paper performs; specimens coated together share one run; separate depositions are separate runs even with the same chemistry, temperature and structure.
specimen: one physical sample that is measured (a coated structure, a film, a substrate piece); it comes from one run.
input entity: a thing that exists before any deposition (a substrate, a porous body), placed under the recipe and named in a deposition activity's inputs.
model run: one set of inputs of a simulation whose result the paper reports (a curve, a table row).
series / replicate / grid (comparisons): never a parent and never asked of the extractor; the compiler derives them from the condition vectors of the experiment units — series: members differ in exactly one quantity; replicate: in none; grid: in two. A name the authors give such a set ("Series A") is kept as its label.
