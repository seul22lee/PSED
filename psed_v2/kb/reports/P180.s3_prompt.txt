pass1_elements.units_block() docstring (verbatim from the source):

    """P148 (A5): PASS-0 UNITS, one line per unit of the latest pass-0 line: id | role | name | parent | data (id:role ...). P161: then
    the units compile.bind() derives (P177b) for the items no pass-0 unit holds, each line ending | <origin>; none, no line."""

Answer to item 3: the recipe's reactant_A / reactant_B / carrier species and the step sequence are NOT given to the model in pass 1. A PASS-0 UNITS line carries id, role, name as printed, parent and data ids with their roles only; pass 0 records no reactant, carrier or step field for a run, and the P179 process_mode fill is not in the block either.
