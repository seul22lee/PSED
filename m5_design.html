Not "only it can solve this" — a well-posed problem an expert (or a good LLM) can also reason about; the loop makes it reproducible, calibrated, and cited.

Caveats & scope

  • Part 1 is a reconstructed subset (12 challenges), ground truth from ALD chemistry; the paper's full 30-challenge list + answers are in its SI. Our score is indicative, not like-for-like vs their 30.
  • The twin is not ground truth. M3: agrees with data in 7/39 profiles; measured 1.91× under-prediction (corrected here). Step-coverage-specific calibration was not separately done — only PD50.
  • LLM = Claude, not o3/GPT-5. Their Table VII numbers are reported, not rerun. A controlled head-to-head needs those models on this exact task.
  • Absolute exposure is uncertain (twin-limited). The robust, measured finding is the AR² scaling difference and the completeness/chemistry/exposure/caveat profile — not the exact Pa·s.
